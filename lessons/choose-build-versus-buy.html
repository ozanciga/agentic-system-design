Build or buy

Questions this lesson answers
  • Managed agent platform or custom runtime: how do you choose?
  • How do you decide whether to build or buy an agent platform?

In short. List what your tasks require (crash recovery, acting on the user's behalf, data-location rules, duplicate-safe tools, testing, monitoring), then test candidate platforms against that list using your own failure cases. Buy what fits; build only where your business rules or security needs demand control. Count the ongoing cost of running whatever you build.

Test what matters, not the feature list

A demo of the happy path tells you little. Run one small end-to-end task on each candidate, and break it on purpose.

Build where control matters

Reuse proven infrastructure where it fits: workflow engines, identity providers, secret managers, monitoring tools. Build what is specific to your business: your tools, policy rules, test cases, and the approval experience.

Include the long-term cost: engineering time, being on call, upgrades, and who will maintain it in two years. Building your own crash recovery or key management is rarely worth it, and the cost never stops.
